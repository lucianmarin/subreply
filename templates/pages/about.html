Install as a web app

  • Install on Android by adding it to your home screen from Chrome.
  • Install on iPhone or iPad by adding it to your home screen from Safari.
  • Also available on Windows, Linux and macOS.

Tech stack and support

  1. Hosted on a high-frequency Vultr VPS running Ubuntu 24.04 LTS.
  2. Built with Python 3.12, Falcon and Django ORM.
  3. Powered by Gunicorn behind Nginx and PgBouncer in front of PostgreSQL.
  4. Icons by Heroicons, Route 159 typeface by Sora Sagano.
  5. Donate via PayPal to keep Subreply independent, ad-free and running indefinitely.

Features and design constraints

  1. Pure reverse-chronological feeds — no algorithmic sorting.
  2. Expressive emoji handles like {{ emo.full_name | emojize }}.
  3. Streamlined, chat-like conversation threads.
  4. Automatic light and dark themes following your OS preference.
  5. First-class support for links, mentions, hashrefs and hashtags.
  6. Focused text: ASCII characters and emojis only.
  7. Concise thoughts: up to 640 characters per reply.
  8. Intentional linking: one link, mention, hashref or hashtag per reply.
  9. Single-paragraph replies to keep conversations clean and readable.
  10. Zero vanity metrics — no likes, follower counts or instant-gratification loops.

Privacy by default

  1. Edit or delete your replies anytime before someone responds.
  2. Remove replies to your posts before responding to them.
  3. Strict GDPR compliance — your data is never sold or redistributed.
  4. Instant, complete account deletion whenever you choose.
  5. Easy data export in a clean, human-readable format.
